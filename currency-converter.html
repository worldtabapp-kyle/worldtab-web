Questions

How up to date are these rates?

They are the reference rates the European Central Bank publishes every working day, and the date above shows which day. TWD, AED and VND, which the ECB does not publish, come from ExchangeRate-API and are also updated daily. They are not live market prices.

Why does my bank show a different amount?

Banks and card networks use their own exchange rate and often add a fee, so the amount on your statement can differ a little from a reference rate.

Can I track spending in several currencies?

Yes. WorldTab records each expense in the currency you paid and shows every total in your currency, keeping the rate from the day you spent.
