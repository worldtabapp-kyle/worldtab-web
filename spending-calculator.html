Questions

Which exchange rate is used?

Today's reference rate for every line. In the WorldTab app, each expense keeps the rate from the day you spent, so totals for past months don't shift when rates move.

Is what I enter saved or sent anywhere?

It is saved only in this browser, so it is still here next time. Nothing you enter is sent to us or anyone else.
