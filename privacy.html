Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Kyle Zhu ("we", "us", "our") handles your information when you use the WorldTab mobile app ("App"). By using the App you agree to this Policy.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information (if you sign in): your email address, a nickname (the one you set before signing in; otherwise your Apple or Google account name, or the part of your email before the @; you can change it in the App), and an avatar emoji.
  • Your financial records: income and expense entries you enter (amount, currency, category, date, note, payment method), ledger names, and the categories, budgets and recurring-entry rules you set up.
  • Receipt photos (optional Premium feature, also available during the free trial): photos you choose to attach to a transaction, stored in Supabase cloud storage.
  • Subscription record (if you subscribe): the store purchase details needed to verify Premium (product, platform, expiry date, renewal status and a purchase token), stored with your account.
  • Free trial start: when your 14-day free Premium trial began, kept on your device and, if you sign in, with your account too, so that reinstalling or switching devices does not restart it.
  • Feedback content: text you submit via "Feedback", and your email address (if signed in) so we can reply to you, together with your app language, level and points, and whether you have Premium.
  • Avatar: only an emoji character you choose — we do not access your camera roll or camera (except for the receipt photo feature, and only when you actively choose to use it).
  • Device and diagnostic data: app version, device model, OS version, and crash logs used to fix bugs.
  • Install attribution data: a device identifier (the Google Advertising ID on Android; the vendor identifier (IDFV) on iOS — we do not request the IDFA and show no tracking permission prompt), the install source, app version, and coarse region, used to tell which advertising channel an install came from.
  • We do not collect precise location, contacts, or browsing history.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide App features (bookkeeping, statistics, currency conversion).
  • To create and secure your account.
  • When you sign in, to back up your records to your account so they can be restored after you switch phones, reinstall or sign out; with Premium, to keep them in sync across your devices.
  • To produce aggregate usage statistics (for example, how many signed-in accounts recorded entries in a given week). These use only the time each entry first reached our servers, not the content of your records.
  • To process subscriptions via Apple App Store or Google Play, and to verify subscription status.
  • To respond to your feedback and support requests, and fix bugs.
  • To use an AI service to parse CSV files you upload into structured bookkeeping entries (Smart Import feature, only triggered when you actively use it).
  • To measure advertising performance: to know which channel an install came from, so we can judge where to keep advertising.

We do not use your bookkeeping content for advertising and do not sell it to third parties. The attribution data above is used only to measure advertising performance and contains none of your records.

3. Data Storage and Retention

  • If you use the App without an account, all entries are stored only on your device.
  • If you sign in, your entries, ledgers, categories, payment methods, budgets and recurring-entry rules are also backed up to servers operated by Supabase, Inc. (supabase.com) in the United States, together with the time each entry first reached our servers. Premium receipt photos are stored there too.
  • When you delete an entry while signed in, it disappears from that device at once. Our server keeps the entry, marked as deleted (as soon as your device is online), so it never comes back in a restore and, with Premium, your other devices remove it the next time they open the App. It is erased completely when you delete your account. To have deleted entries erased sooner, email support@worldtab.app (we will first check that none of your other devices still holds the entry).
  • Account data is retained while your account is active. Upon account deletion, data is permanently removed within 30 days, unless retention is required by law.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • With Supabase, Inc. for cloud storage, authentication, and receipt photo storage (under a data processing agreement).
  • With Apple Inc. / Google LLC to process subscription payments and verify subscription status.
  • With Resend (resend.com) to deliver the feedback content you submit to our inbox.
  • With Anthropic PBC (Claude API) to parse CSV file content you actively upload for Smart Import; this content is not used to train models.
  • With AppsFlyer Ltd. (appsflyer.com) for the install attribution data described above, to measure advertising performance.
  • With Functional Software, Inc. (Sentry, sentry.io) for crash and error diagnostics, to locate and fix bugs.
  • With Vercel Inc., which hosts the service we use to verify App Store purchases (purchase details only).
  • Exchange rates are downloaded from api.frankfurter.dev, and the Taiwan dollar, UAE dirham and Vietnamese dong rates from open.er-api.com (ExchangeRate-API). Like any website, these services see your device's IP address, but no account or bookkeeping data is sent to them.
  • When required by applicable law or to protect our legal rights.

5. Security

We use industry-standard measures including encryption in transit (TLS) and access controls. No method of internet transmission is 100% secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights

You may at any time:

  • Get a copy of your data: Premium members (including during the free trial) can export their entries as CSV (Settings → Data & Backup → Export CSV), and anyone can email support@worldtab.app for a free copy of everything we hold about them.
  • Correct your profile (Settings → tap your profile card at the top → Account).
  • Delete your account and all associated data (Settings → Account → Delete Account).
  • Contact us for other requests: support@worldtab.app.

7. European Union Users (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Right of access, rectification, and erasure ("right to be forgotten").
  • Right to data portability (a free copy of your data on request, or CSV export in the App for Premium members).
  • Right to restrict or object to processing.
  • Right to withdraw consent at any time (where processing is based on consent).
  • Right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

The legal bases for processing are: contract performance (operating the App), legitimate interests (security, bug fixes, aggregate usage statistics, and measuring advertising performance), and consent (optional account creation). We do not transfer data outside the EEA without appropriate safeguards (Supabase's Standard Contractual Clauses apply).

8. Canada (PIPEDA)

We handle personal information in accordance with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA). You may request access to or correction of your personal information by contacting support@worldtab.app.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have done so inadvertently, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

10. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in the United States (where Supabase servers are located). We ensure appropriate protections are in place consistent with this Policy and applicable law.

11. Changes to This Policy

We will post updates with a revised "Last updated" date. We will notify you of material changes through the App. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated Policy.
